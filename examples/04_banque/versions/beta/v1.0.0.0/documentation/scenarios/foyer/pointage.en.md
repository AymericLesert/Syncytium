When: whenever the bank statement arrives.

1. Open [the entries without a posting date](banque.ecriture[pointage]) and choose the account.
2. For each line of the statement, set the posting date directly in the list: the line leaves the
   list of entries to reconcile.
3. A statement line missing from the list is entered: [create an entry](banque.ecriture[+record])
   with its transaction date, its posting date, its description, its budget, its amount.
4. At the end, open [the entries by posting date](banque.ecriture[par_comptable]) and export the
   statement as PDF: [the printed entries receive their printing date](banque.ecriture.creer_releve).

> **Do not**: set a posting date on an entry that is not on the statement — it will appear on the
> next one, and that is where it gets reconciled.
